## Key precedent (Kraken Financial)

- Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City approval of Kraken Financial's
  Limited Purpose Master Account, March 4, 2026.
- One-year initial term; Fedwire access; NO interest on reserves;
  NO discount window access; Tier 3 review under the Aug 2022 Account
  Access Guidelines.
- Crypto Valley Journal coverage:
  https://cryptovalleyjournal.com/focus/legal-and-compliance/kraken-becomes-first-crypto-firm-to-gain-direct-access-to-the-federal-reserve-system/
