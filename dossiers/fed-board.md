# Federal Reserve Board + Reserve Bank presidents: conflict-of-interest dossier

*Compiled May 26, 2026 for the EO 14405 stablecoin-contagion working paper. All concrete claims carry an inline source URL. Items the open record cannot settle are flagged as "Unverified" rather than guessed.*

## Executive summary

Two figures dominate the conflict surface for Executive Order 14405's master-account review. **Kevin Warsh**, sworn in as Fed Chair on May 13, 2026, brought to the chair both the highest disclosed personal wealth in modern Fed history (over $100M in publicly visible assets, with at least $100M more shielded behind Duquesne Family Office confidentiality agreements that an OGE ethics officer flagged as out of compliance) and direct equity stakes in more than twenty crypto and blockchain ventures ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/kevin-warsh-net-worth-fed-chair-nominee/); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/14/the-next-fed-chair-has-a-crypto-portfolio-here-s-everything-that-s-in-it)). **Jeffrey Schmid**, Kansas City Fed President, signed off on the Kraken Financial limited-purpose master account on March 4, 2026, an action with no clear statutory anchor in the Federal Reserve Act ([Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/kraken-receives-fed-master-account-crypto-skinny-account-custodia/813814/); [Waters letter](https://democrats-financialservices.house.gov/uploadedfiles/fed_kraken_master_account.pdf)). A third figure, **Christopher Waller**, is the policy architect of the "skinny master account" idea EO 14405 effectively endorses ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2025/10/21/gov-waller-u-s-fed-to-embrace-disruption-pitches-skinny-master-account-idea)). At the other end of the spectrum, **Neel Kashkari** publicly called stablecoins "buzzword salad" and crypto "utterly useless" in February 2026, the loudest dissenter on the current FOMC ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/02/19/fed-s-neel-kashkari-calls-crypto-utterly-useless-dismisses-stablecoins-as-buzzword-salad)). The composition of the Board now skews materially toward the crypto-permissive view that EO 14405 was designed to ratify.

## Methodology

Primary sources used: Federal Reserve Board and Reserve Bank official biographies; OGE Form 278 disclosures as reported by major outlets; speeches archived on federalreserve.gov and individual Reserve Bank sites; the Senate Banking Committee record; House Financial Services correspondence (Waters letter on Kraken); Wikipedia entries cross-checked against primary sources. Limits: full OGE-278 PDFs are not always public; spouse and family employment is rarely officially disclosed and was searched only through public reporting. Where a claim could not be substantiated, it is omitted or labeled Unverified. Net-worth figures from `celebritynetworth.com` are flagged as low-confidence.

## Board of Governors

### Kevin Warsh (Chair, confirmed May 13, 2026)

- **Background**: Born 1970, Harvard Law 1995. Confirmed Fed Chair 54-45 on May 13, 2026, the closest modern vote for the position; Democrat John Fetterman crossed over ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/13/nx-s1-5816235/kevin-warsh-federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell)).
- **Prior employment**:
  - Morgan Stanley M&A division, 1995-2002, rising to executive director ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Warsh)).
  - White House National Economic Council, special assistant to Bush 43, 2002-2006 ([Federal Reserve History](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/people/kevin-m-warsh)).
  - Federal Reserve Board of Governors, 2006-2011.
  - Duquesne Family Office (Stanley Druckenmiller's personal vehicle), partner/advisor 2011-2026 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/kevin-warsh-family-office.html)).
  - Hoover Institution, Shepard Family Distinguished Visiting Fellow in Economics, 2011-2026; Stanford GSB lecturer ([Hoover](https://www.hoover.org/news/hoover-institution-congratulates-kevin-warsh-confirmation-federal-reserve-chair)).
- **External affiliations**: Hoover (resigned upon confirmation). Stanford GSB. G30 member ([Hoover G30 lecture](https://www.hoover.org/research/g30-spring-lecture-2025-kevin-warsh-commanding-heights-central-banks-crossroads)).
- **Family ties to financial services / crypto**: Married Jane Lauder, granddaughter of Estée Lauder, in 2002. Jane Lauder has been on Estée Lauder Companies' board since 2009 and was promoted to Global President of Origins/Darphin/Ojon in 2013 ([Wikipedia: Jane Lauder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Lauder)). Estée Lauder is not a financial-services firm, but the marital household's beneficial exposure to Estée Lauder common stock is the dominant component of disclosed wealth. No crypto-industry affiliation of a family member is documented in the open record.
- **Disclosed assets**: April 2026 OGE-278 disclosed over $100M in personal assets, including two stakes worth $50M+ each in Duquesne's "Juggernaut Fund." Warsh declined to itemize Juggernaut's underlying holdings citing pre-existing confidentiality agreements; the OGE ethics officer marked him out of compliance, with a divestiture pledge of 90 days post-confirmation ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/kevin-warsh-net-worth-fed-chair-nominee/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/16/fed-kevin-warsh-elizabeth-warren-financial-disclosures.html)). He received $10.2M in consulting fees from Duquesne in the prior twelve months ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/fed-nominee-kevin-warsh-assets-100-million-rcna331689)). Senator Warren sent Druckenmiller a formal request to release Warsh from the confidentiality agreements ([Senate Banking](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/warren-presses-druckenmiller-to-release-kevin-warsh-from-confidentiality-agreements-on-undisclosed-100-million)). CoinDesk's review of the same filing identifies equity positions in 20+ blockchain and digital-asset companies "spanning DeFi lending, decentralized derivatives, Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks, prediction markets, and Bitcoin payments infrastructure" ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/14/the-next-fed-chair-has-a-crypto-portfolio-here-s-everything-that-s-in-it)). Warsh pledged to divest conflicting positions.
- **Public position on EO 14405 / master accounts**: Opposes a retail CBDC, supports private stablecoins, has signaled openness to limited Fed access for stablecoin issuers and trust-chartered nonbanks ([Blockhead](https://www.blockhead.co/2026/05/14/kevin-warsh-is-fed-chair-bitcoin-payments-equity-anti-cbdc-stance-first-meeting-in-june/)). Has framed Bitcoin as a useful policy discipline mechanism (a "good policeman for policy").
- **Industry speaking engagements**: Hoover G30 Spring Lecture 2025, "Commanding Heights: Central Banks At A Crossroads" ([Hoover](https://www.hoover.org/research/g30-spring-lecture-2025-kevin-warsh-commanding-heights-central-banks-crossroads)). Pre-Fed speaking footprint at Hoover, Stanford GSB, and CEPR-affiliated venues is extensive; a comprehensive industry-conference list (Consensus, Bitcoin Miami) is **Unverified** from public sources.
- **Conflict-of-interest assessment**: Highest documented exposure on the Board. The combination of (1) undisclosed Juggernaut holdings, (2) twenty-plus disclosed crypto-venture equity stakes, (3) a Duquesne consulting income stream of $10M+, and (4) decision authority over the precise asset class he holds equity in places Warsh in the most acute conflict position of any sitting Fed Chair in the modern record. The divestiture pledge mitigates but does not eliminate the appearance issue.
- **Sources**: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Warsh); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/kevin-warsh-net-worth-fed-chair-nominee/); [CNBC family office](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/kevin-warsh-family-office.html); [Senate Banking - Warren](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/warren-presses-druckenmiller-to-release-kevin-warsh-from-confidentiality-agreements-on-undisclosed-100-million); [CoinDesk crypto portfolio](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/14/the-next-fed-chair-has-a-crypto-portfolio-here-s-everything-that-s-in-it); [NPR confirmation](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/13/nx-s1-5816235/kevin-warsh-federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell); [Hoover Institution profile](https://www.hoover.org/profiles/kevin-warsh).

### Christopher Waller

- **Background**: Born 1959 in Nebraska City, NE. BS Bemidji State; MA, PhD Washington State (economics) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Waller)). Confirmed as Fed Governor December 2020 on Trump 1.0 nomination.
- **Prior employment**: EVP and Director of Research, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, 2009-2020. Earlier academic posts at Notre Dame (2003-2009), Kentucky (1998-2003), Indiana (1985-1998) ([LegiStorm](https://www.legistorm.com/person/bio/390885/Christopher_J_Waller.html)).
- **External affiliations**: None in active financial-services boards in the public record.
- **Family ties to financial services / crypto**: Not in the public record. Spouse identity is **Unverified** in open sources.
- **Disclosed assets**: Not surfaced in the search; subsequent OGE-278 review needed.
- **Public position on EO 14405 / master accounts**: Author of the "skinny master account" proposal of October 2025 that EO 14405 ratifies. Waller has explicitly stated "If stablecoins present a lower cost alternative to consumers and businesses, I am all for it" and that the Fed should "embrace disruption" ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2025/10/21/gov-waller-u-s-fed-to-embrace-disruption-pitches-skinny-master-account-idea); [Federal Reserve speech](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20251021a.htm)). He has nonetheless flagged that stablecoin "rewards" programs are "skirting the spirit of the law" against interest-bearing deposits ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2025/federal-reserve-governor-christopher-waller-private-sector-better-able-innovate-stablecoins/)). His February 2025 stablecoin speech ([Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20250212a.htm)) is the most cited Board-level statement on private dollar tokens.
- **Industry speaking engagements**: Spoke at the 2025 Payments Innovation Conference; cited at Cato monetary conferences in prior years.
- **Conflict-of-interest assessment**: Policy conflict rather than financial conflict. Waller is the intellectual author of the framework EO 14405 codifies; he will both vote on and shape its implementation. No documented personal financial interest in stablecoin or crypto firms in the public record.
- **Sources**: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Waller); [Waller stablecoin speech 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20250212a.htm); [Waller payments speech October 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20251021a.htm); [PYMNTS coverage](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2025/federal-reserve-governor-christopher-waller-private-sector-better-able-innovate-stablecoins/).

- **Conflict-of-interest assessment**: Hudson Bay Capital's FTX-claims trading is the relevant industry tie; OGE filing review would clarify whether any positions remained at the time of his Fed vote. Policy alignment with EO 14405 is the strongest in the open record after Warsh and Waller.

## Uncertainty

The following items could not be verified to a primary-source standard in this pass and would benefit from follow-up:

- **Spouse and immediate-family employment** for Waller, Bowman, Cook, Jefferson, Barr, Williams, Collins, Paulson, Hammack, Barkin, Goolsbee, Musalem, Kashkari, Schmid, Logan, Daly. Public-records searches did not surface confirmed crypto- or fintech-industry employment for any spouse. The negative finding is not the same as a positive disclaimer; full OGE-278 review is the right next step.
- **Full OGE Form 278 disclosures** for governors and reserve bank presidents are not always public; CBS News and CoinDesk reporting on Warsh is unusually granular because of the high-profile confirmation. Comparable depth on Waller, Bowman, Jefferson, Barr, Miran, and most regional presidents would require pulling OGE filings via FOIA or, where available, the OGE Public Financial Disclosure portal.
- **Specific industry-conference attendance** (Consensus, Bitcoin Miami, ETHDenver, DC Blockchain Summit) by Fed officials was not exhaustively mapped. Worth a targeted speaker-list pull for the 2022-2026 events.
- **Beth Hammack's family ties**, given the depth of her Goldman tenure and TBAC chair role, are an obvious next target for documentation.
- **Stephen Miran's specific Hudson Bay Capital portfolio holdings** at the time of his Fed vote are not detailed in the open record; the firm's FTX-claims trading is documented but his personal book is not.
- **Kraken Financial's specific bilateral counterparties** at the Kansas City Fed in the period immediately surrounding the March 4, 2026 approval would inform whether the pilot is operationally a stablecoin reserve-management vehicle or a payments vehicle in the narrow sense.
- **Patrick Harker's** post-Philadelphia-Fed affiliations after June 30, 2025 retirement are not surveyed here.
- **Christopher Waller's spouse** is not identified in the public sources reviewed. The biographical sources that came up in search appear to confuse him with several other Christopher Wallers (a horse trainer, an athlete) and would require careful documentary work.
