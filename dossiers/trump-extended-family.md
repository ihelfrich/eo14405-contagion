# Trump extended family financial and crypto exposure

## Executive summary

This dossier documents the financial activities, board positions, and foreign-state exposure of Trump extended family members and romantic partners not covered in the parallel WLFI/USD1 dossier (which already addresses Donald, Eric, Don Jr, and Barron Trump). The targets are Jared Kushner, Ivanka Trump, Tiffany Trump, Lara Trump, Kimberly Guilfoyle, Michael Boulos, Massad Boulos, Robert Trump's estate, and Don Jr's adult-age children. The single largest financial vehicle in this group is **Affinity Partners** (A Fin Management LLC, 100% owned by Jared Kushner), a Miami-based private fund adviser whose Form ADV filed March 22, 2026 reports $6.16 billion in assets under management, approximately 99% of which is sourced from non-US investors concentrated in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the UAE ([Affinity Partners Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_Partners); [Senator Wyden letter, Sept 24, 2024](https://www.finance.senate.gov/chairmans-news/wyden-investigation-of-kushner-firm-continues-new-letter-outlines-affinity-partners-fee-structure-lack-of-return-to-investors-questionable-deals-with-foreign-governments)). The second nexus of interest is the **Boulos family** (Massad and Michael), whose claimed Lebanese-Nigerian conglomerate wealth has been challenged in primary reporting by the New York Times. Lara Trump declined the open North Carolina Senate seat in July 2025 and remains a Fox News host; Kimberly Guilfoyle was confirmed as US Ambassador to Greece on September 18, 2025 and is no longer engaged to Don Jr. Ivanka Trump's principal disclosed economic interest is as a partner in Affinity Partners (her husband's firm) and as a participant in the Sazan Island Albania redevelopment. No member of this extended group has been documented in any SEC filing as a beneficial owner of DT Marks DEFI LLC (the Trump-family WLFI vehicle), but the Boulos and Kushner Middle East connections overlap with WLFI's largest disclosed customer (UAE-linked MGX, $2 billion USD1 purchase). Conflict-of-interest exposure for EO 14405 analysis is therefore indirect for this group, mediated through the same Gulf sovereign-fund relationships that drive WLFI demand.

## Jared Kushner and Affinity Partners

### Corporate vehicle

The investment adviser of record is **A Fin Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company founded in 2021 and solely owned by Jared Kushner; the trade name is **Affinity Partners** ([Affinity Investment Group ADV Part 2A, March 13, 2025](https://www.affinityinvestmentgroup.com/files/adv_part_2a_final_03.13.2025_affinity.pdf)). Principal place of business: Miami, Florida. The most recent Form ADV amendment on file was dated **March 22, 2026** for fiscal year ending December 31, 2025 ([Newstracs summary of March 2026 ADV](https://newstracs.com/jared-kushners-affinity-partners-investment-firm-filed-a-disclosure-showing-over-six-billion-aum/2026/03/23/)).

### Assets under management

The AUM trajectory disclosed across successive Form ADV filings:
- December 31, 2022: **$3.055 billion** discretionary
- 2024: **$4.8 billion** following Qatar Investment Authority commitment
- December 31, 2025 (per March 2026 ADV): **$6.16 billion** across approximately 6 clients ([Investing.com / Affinity AUM growth, 2024](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/affinity-partners-grows-assets-to-48-billion-with-middle-east-funding-93CH-3954437); Newstracs summary)

### LP composition

The Wyden Senate Finance Committee letter of September 24, 2024 reports the **Affinity Partners Parallel Fund I LP** as **100% non-US-person owned** with **six foreign beneficial owners**. The principal disclosed LPs are:
- **Saudi Public Investment Fund**: $2 billion commitment (2021, approved over PIF screening committee objections) ([Affinity Partners Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_Partners))
- **Qatar Investment Authority**: approximately $1.5 billion (2024), co-invested with Lunate
- **Lunate** (Abu Dhabi-based asset manager): co-investor with Qatar in 2024 round
- Three additional foreign beneficial owners (identities not publicly disclosed in the Wyden materials) [uncertain]

### Fee structure

Per the Wyden letter:
- Saudi PIF pays **1.25% annual management fee** on its $2 billion commitment, equating to approximately $25 million per year regardless of investment performance
- Other LPs pay approximately **2% annual** management fee
- Cumulative management fees collected through mid-2024: **approximately $157 million**, including **approximately $87 million from the Saudi government**
- Returns to investors as of July 2024: **zero distributed earnings** (fund-performance line items marked "N/A") ([Wyden letter Sept 24, 2024](https://www.finance.senate.gov/chairmans-news/wyden-investigation-of-kushner-firm-continues-new-letter-outlines-affinity-partners-fee-structure-lack-of-return-to-investors-questionable-deals-with-foreign-governments))

The Wyden letter characterizes the 1.25% Saudi fee as "unusually high" for a first-time manager with no prior track record, and flags that LP commitment agreements **expire August 2026**, placing renewal negotiations inside Trump's second term.

## Conflict-of-interest assessment relevant to EO 14405

EO 14405 (91 FR 30475, May 22, 2026) directs federal banking regulators to roll back rules restricting non-bank financial companies and digital-asset firms from Federal Reserve master-account access and payment services. The extended-family group documented here has three indirect exposure channels to that policy:

1. **Shared sovereign-wealth LP base.** Affinity Partners' $6.16 billion AUM is approximately 99% non-US-investor money, concentrated in Saudi PIF, Qatar Investment Authority, and Abu Dhabi-linked Lunate. WLFI's largest disclosed USD1 use case (MGX's $2 billion Binance settlement, May 2025) is an Abu Dhabi state-backed transaction. The Affinity LP roster expires for renewal in August 2026, inside the Trump 2.0 term. The conflict structure is not that Affinity holds USD1, but that the same Gulf principals who pay Kushner management fees are USD1's largest customers and prospective customers ([Wyden letter](https://www.finance.senate.gov/chairmans-news/wyden-investigation-of-kushner-firm-continues-new-letter-outlines-affinity-partners-fee-structure-lack-of-return-to-investors-questionable-deals-with-foreign-governments); [WLFI dossier this project](trump-wlfi.md)).

2. **OakNorth bank-charter adjacency.** Affinity's August 2025 ~$200 million stake in OakNorth Bank, a UK SME lender pursuing US expansion, places Kushner at the table for any UK or US fintech-bank policy shift. EO 14405's Section 4 directive on non-bank access to Reserve Bank payment services is the precise regulatory perimeter that would matter to a US-expanding fintech bank. Direct USD1 partnership with OakNorth is not documented [uncertain].

3. **Policy access via Massad Boulos.** Boulos's dual Senior Advisor roles (Arab and Middle East Affairs since January 2025; Africa since April 2025) place a Trump in-law inside two regional policy portfolios in which Gulf sovereign-fund flows materially affect WLFI and USD1 demand. The Wyden letter explicitly identifies the "foreign-government leverage on family finances" channel; the Boulos roles instantiate the inverse direction (family member exerting leverage on US policy toward those same governments).

The extended family's exposure is therefore **structurally indirect but quantitatively large**. The Affinity Partners management-fee stream (approximately $25 million per year from Saudi PIF alone, plus approximately 2% from non-Saudi LPs aggregating to roughly $80-120 million per year on $6 billion of AUM) is the single largest documented current-period cash flow from foreign sovereign wealth to a Trump in-law's household. WLFI/USD1 cash flows accrue to a separate Trump-family vehicle (DT Marks DEFI LLC), but both pools draw from the same Gulf sovereign-wealth fund family. EO 14405 does not directly enrich Affinity Partners; it enriches USD1, whose float-revenue ultimately flows back to the Trump family through the same Gulf-sourced demand channel that Affinity's fund-renewal cycle depends on.

The remaining three beneficial owners of Parallel Fund I LP are not disclosed in any public Form ADV section retrieved here and would require either an unredacted Wyden Finance Committee response or PACER/state-court litigation discovery to confirm.
- **Specific direct USD1/WLFI partnership with any Affinity portfolio company.** OakNorth Bank is the closest structural adjacency but no public USD1-partnership announcement was located. The link asserted in this dossier is structural (shared Gulf LP base) not contractual.
- **Michael Boulos's actual current role at SCOA Nigeria.** Secondary press attributes the CEO title to him; the NYT investigation attributes it to his father Massad. The Nigerian Corporate Affairs Commission registry would resolve this but was not retrieved in this pass.
- **Massad Boulos's $1.53 personal SCOA stake figure.** The figure appears in NYT investigation summaries as reported in Naharnet and Ya Libnan. Whether this is a unit-rounding artifact or a substantively correct nominal-share figure requires retrieval of SCOA Nigeria's 2024 annual report from the Nigerian Stock Exchange.
- **Robert Trump's estate's connection to current Trump Organization or WLFI ownership.** Estate plan terms are not public; the relationship between the estate's residue and DT Marks DEFI LLC's equity stack is undocumented in EDGAR filings.
- **Don Jr's adult children's holdings.** Kai Trump's NIL valuation and Accelerator Active Energy equity are reported but unverified against any required disclosure (none applies; she is a private college student). Donald J. Trump III turned 17 in February 2026 and is not subject to any disclosed exposure.
- **Affinity Partners August 2026 LP renewal terms.** The Wyden letter flags this as the conflict horizon; whether Saudi PIF, QIA, or Lunate intend to renew, and on what fee structure, is not publicly disclosed.
- **Whether Ivanka Trump or any other extended-family member is among the unidentified Trump-family beneficial owners of DT Marks DEFI LLC** (which holds approximately 38% of WLF Holdco LLC and 75% of WLF net protocol revenue per the companion dossier). The 30% non-Donald-Trump share of DT Marks DEFI LLC is attributed in press reports to "Trump family members" without itemized identification.
