# World Liberty Financial / USD1 / Trump family

## Executive summary

World Liberty Financial, Inc. (WLF) is a Delaware non-stock corporation launched in September 2024, controlled through a stack of holding companies in which Trump-family-owned DT Marks DEFI LLC holds an equity stake (variously disclosed as 60%, 40%, and most recently approximately 38% of parent WLF Holdco LLC) and a contractual right to 75% of net protocol revenues ([CNBC, Oct 17, 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/10/17/trump-crypto-project-allows-ex-president-family-to-make-75percent-of-revenue.html); [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial)). WLF issues two assets: the WLFI governance token (100 billion supply cap, of which 22.5 billion are allocated to Trump-family-affiliated entities) and the USD1 stablecoin (launched March 2025, custodied by BitGo Trust Company, reserves managed by BlackRock and reported above \$2 billion circulating supply by late 2025 and above \$4 billion by April 2026) ([CoinMarketCap profile](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/usd1/what-is/); [eco.com support](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11753300-usd1-stablecoin-by-world-liberty-financial)). On May 19, 2026, President Trump signed Executive Order 14405, "Integrating Financial Technology Innovation Into Regulatory Frameworks" (91 FR 30475, published May 22, 2026), which directs federal banking regulators and the Federal Reserve to identify and roll back rules restricting non-bank financial companies (including digital-asset firms) from accessing Reserve Bank payment accounts and payment services ([Federal Register listing](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/22/2026-10399/integrating-financial-technology-innovation-into-regulatory-frameworks); [White House page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/05/integrating-financial-technology-innovation-into-regulatory-frameworks/)). The structural conflict is direct: the EO's beneficiary class includes the issuer of a stablecoin whose economics flow back to the sitting president's family.

## Corporate structure

The SEC Form D/A filed July 3, 2025 (accession 0002043140-25-000001, CIK 0002043140) lists the issuer as **World Liberty Financial, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation incorporated in 2024, with principal place of business at 4400 Biscayne Boulevard, Suite 900, Miami, FL 33137, phone 212-672-4700 ([Form D/A, SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0002043140)). Related persons named on the filing are **Zachary Folkman** (Executive Officer, Director, Promoter; listed address in Dorado, Puerto Rico) and **Chase Herro** (same combined roles). The filing reports a total offering amount of **\$52,133,139**, total amount sold **\$52,133,139**, and **1,966 investors** as of the amendment date, with a clarifying note that those totals "exclude amounts offered and sold to non-US persons in a separate concurrent offering consistent with Regulation S" (Form D/A, Item 13).

Above the operating corporation sits **WLF Holdco LLC**, a Delaware LLC that holds the sole membership interest in WLF, Inc. Above Holdco sits **DT Marks DEFI LLC**, a Delaware entity in which public reporting attributes 70% ownership to Donald J. Trump and 30% to unidentified Trump family members ([CNBC, Oct 17, 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/10/17/trump-crypto-project-allows-ex-president-family-to-make-75percent-of-revenue.html); [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial)). DT Marks DEFI LLC's disclosed equity in WLF Holdco has shifted: initially reported as 60%, reduced to roughly 40% during the stablecoin push in 2025, and stated as approximately 38% in later disclosures ([The Block, 2025](https://www.theblock.co/post/358892/trump-linked-firm-quietly-reduces-20-of-its-equity-interests-in-world-liberty-financial); [Cryptonews, 2025](https://cryptonews.com/news/world-liberty-financial-ownership-shake-up-trump-family-cuts-stake-during-stablecoin-push/)).

A related entity, **World Liberty Trust**, applied for a national bank trust charter with the OCC in January 2026 [secondary] ([Yahoo Finance / CoinDesk, Jan 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/trump-linked-world-liberty-financial-231604023.html)).

USD1 reserve custody is held at **BitGo Trust Company**, a South Dakota-chartered trust company; reserve management is reported as **BlackRock** institutional money market products (short-duration Treasury bills) with a smaller cash deposit slice at qualified US banks ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11753300-usd1-stablecoin-by-world-liberty-financial); [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/usd1/what-is/)).

## Token economics

**WLFI token.** Supply cap 100 billion. Initial release on September 16, 2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial)). Original public sale target \$300 million; Form D filed October 30, 2024 reset the target to \$30 million after the initial sale underperformed ([CoinGape, 2024](https://coingape.com/donald-trumps-world-liberty-financial-lowers-300m-public-sale-target/); SEC Form D, accession 0002043140-24-000002). The cumulative amount reported sold on the July 2025 Form D/A is **\$52.13 million** for the US-person Regulation D tranche, with a separate Regulation S tranche to non-US persons. Trump-family-affiliated entities were allocated **22.5 billion WLFI** of the 100 billion total (22.5% of supply) ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/369026/trump-family-crypto-wealth-wlfi-token-debut)). Following exchange listing in September 2025, secondary press estimates valued the Trump-family-affiliated WLFI position at roughly **\$5 billion on paper**; Forbes estimated by March 2026 that Trump had netted **\$550 million from token sales** [secondary] ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/369026/trump-family-crypto-wealth-wlfi-token-debut); [Forbes via Cryptonews](https://cryptonews.com/news/world-liberty-financial-ownership-shake-up-trump-family-cuts-stake-during-stablecoin-push/)).

**USD1 stablecoin.** Launched March 2025 on Ethereum and BNB Chain ([eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/11753300-usd1-stablecoin-by-world-liberty-financial)). Pegged 1:1 to USD. Reserves disclosed as cash plus short-duration US Treasury bills managed by BlackRock, custody at BitGo. Circulating supply: reported at approximately **\$2 billion by late 2025** and above **\$4 billion by April 2026** ([CoinMarketCap profile](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/usd1/what-is/)). Revenue model: float-on-reserves, with annual revenue from Treasury and money-market interest reported at roughly **\$80 million per year** [secondary] ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial)).

**Notable USD1 demand event.** In May 2025, MGX, an Abu Dhabi state-backed investment vehicle chaired by Sheikh Tahnoun bin Zayed Al Nahyan, announced it would use **\$2 billion of USD1** to settle a strategic investment into Binance, providing the largest single use case for the stablecoin and a corresponding boost to reserve float income flowing back to WLF ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial); [Public Citizen brief](https://www.citizen.org/article/trump-crypto-world-liberty-financial-binance-iran-sanctions/)).

## EO 14405 conflict assessment

EO 14405, "Integrating Financial Technology Innovation Into Regulatory Frameworks," published at **91 FR 30475 (May 22, 2026)**, document number 2026-10399, contains the following operative provisions material to USD1:

- **Section 3 (Regulatory Review).** Each federal financial regulator must, within 90 days, identify regulations, guidance documents, orders, and no-action letters that "unduly impede fintech firms" from partnering with regulated institutions or obtaining federal charters, licenses, registrations, and authorizations ([Consumer Finance Monitor analysis, May 21, 2026](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/05/21/white-house-executive-order-signals-major-shift-in-federal-policy-for-fintechs-and-payment-systems/)).
- **Section 4 (Federal Reserve Access).** The Federal Reserve is directed to conduct, within 120 days, a comprehensive evaluation of legal and policy frameworks governing access to Reserve Bank payment accounts and payment services by uninsured depository institutions and non-bank financial companies, including digital asset firms ([Consumer Finance Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/05/21/white-house-executive-order-signals-major-shift-in-federal-policy-for-fintechs-and-payment-systems/); [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/05/integrating-financial-technology-innovation-into-regulatory-frameworks/)).
- **Section 5 (180-day implementation).** Regulators must "take steps to encourage innovation" consistent with their reviews.

**Why this benefits USD1 specifically.** USD1 is custodied at a state-chartered (South Dakota) trust company, BitGo Trust, which is precisely the "uninsured depository institution / non-bank financial company" class for which Section 4 directs Fed re-evaluation of master-account and payment-services access. The GENIUS Act (enacted July 2025) requires payment stablecoin issuers to hold reserves in cash and short-dated Treasuries and to be supervised by a federal or qualified state regulator. The combination of (i) GENIUS-Act-compliant reserve structure, (ii) trust-company custody, and (iii) a forthcoming Federal Reserve review of non-bank master-account access, materially lowers USD1's settlement frictions and reduces its dependence on correspondent commercial banks. The same mechanics would not have benefited Tether (offshore, non-GENIUS-compliant); they benefit Circle (USDC) and USD1 most directly, with USD1 the smallest of the three and therefore the largest proportional beneficiary.

The conflict structure is therefore not a question of an EO that names USD1. It is that EO 14405 modifies the regulatory chassis on which USD1's business model rests, and the president signing the order has, through DT Marks DEFI LLC's contractual interest in WLF Holdco LLC, an economic claim on USD1 reserve float income. Public Citizen and the House investigators have characterized the broader pattern as a conflict of interest; the specific channel here is the float-revenue channel ([Public Citizen, 2026](https://www.citizen.org/article/trump-crypto-world-liberty-financial-binance-iran-sanctions/); [CoinDesk House probe](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/02/05/house-probe-targets-wlfi-after-report-of-usd500-million-uae-stake)).

## Uncertainty

- **Form D current totals beyond July 2025.** The most recent Form D/A on file (accession 0002043140-25-000001, filed July 3, 2025) reports \$52,133,139 in cumulative Reg-D sales. WLF has reportedly raised more since via Reg-S and other vehicles; the SEC-disclosed US-person total of record is the \$52.1 million figure. Verification of post-July 2025 raise amounts requires further Reg-S documentation not on EDGAR.
- **Exact federal docket number for Sun v. WLF.** Press reporting names the parties and the substance but the docket has not been confirmed against CourtListener/RECAP in this pass. Verification requires direct PACER or CourtListener search by case caption.
- **UAE \$500 million 49% stake.** Reported by WSJ and tracked by House investigators but not confirmed in any SEC filing; closing terms and counterparties remain [uncertain].
- **OGE Form 278e disclosures (Donald J. Trump, 2025 annual).** Cited by the Center for American Progress methodology document but not directly retrieved in this dossier; the PDF was rendered as binary and could not be parsed. The OGE filing should be retrieved directly from oge.gov for any income figures quoted in a paper.
- **Exact quoted operative text of EO 14405, Sections 3-5.** Verbatim quotation requires retrieving the GovInfo PDF in readable form; this dossier relies on the Consumer Finance Monitor legal analysis for the section-by-section paraphrase.
- **USD1 reserve attestation reports.** Existence and identity of an attestation auditor (analogous to Circle's Deloitte engagement for USDC) was not confirmed. Required for any reserve-quality claim beyond what WLF self-discloses.
- **WLFI as an unregistered security.** The Duke FinReg analysis is academic argument; no SEC enforcement action confirming this characterization exists as of this dossier's compilation date.
